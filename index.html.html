Our Story

Tradition Served
with Elegance

Kailash Parbat Vizag brings the timeless spirit of North Indian vegetarian cuisine to Beach Road — a destination built on legendary street-food recipes, comforting classics and a genuine warmth that turns every visit into an occasion.

Every dish carries forward recipes passed down through generations, reimagined with contemporary hospitality and served in a setting designed for celebration, family and quiet indulgence alike.

Our Signatures

Flavours Worth Coming Back For

From legendary street-food favourites to comforting North Indian classics.

Pani Puri

Crisp semolina shells, tangy tamarind water and spiced chickpeas — Vizag's favourite ritual, elevated.

Chole Bhature

Slow-simmered spiced chickpeas with pillowy fried bread — a North Indian classic done right.

Chaat

Tangy chutneys, crunchy sev and fresh yogurt in every bite.

Paneer Specialities

North Indian Thali

Dal, sabzi, roti, rice and accompaniments, served the traditional way.

Desserts
